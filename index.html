How VLMs See Depth RESEARCH

AI research · PyTorch · three.js · in progress

Do vision-language models use depth cues the way people do? A seeded 3D renderer and adaptive Bayesian staircases (Psi method) measure how VLMs weigh five depth cues, validated on simulated observers and run across nine models. Qwen2.5-VL-3B reads relative size backwards (p ≈ 7×10⁻¹², unchanged at fp16 — not a quantisation artefact); the 7B sees all five cues but judges depth from height alone.
